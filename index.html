Transfer this protocol to another scanner

Builds a protocol on the target scanner with the same image noise as your current settings, using the nearest available kV, rotation time, pitch, collimation, kernel and reconstruction, and the target vendor's AEC setting.

Model and limits

  • Dose: CTDIvol = nCTDIw(scanner, phantom, 120 kV) × relative tube output at the chosen kV (0.32 at 80 kV, 0.62 at 100 kV, 1.44 at 140 kV) × effective mAs/100 × overbeaming factor. Effective mAs = mA × rotation time / pitch, averaged over the scan range when AEC modulates the tube current. DLP = CTDIvol × (scan length + helical overranging) × number of phases. E = DLP × k (AAPM Report 96). SSDE: AAPM TG-204 (body) and TG-293 (head).
  • AEC: Siemens CARE Dose4D uses the quality reference mAs (QRM) for a reference adult, with average-strength adaptation to size. Philips DoseRight uses the Dose Right Index (each step ≈ 12% mAs). GE SmartmA keeps a target noise index at the first reconstruction slice thickness. Canon SUREExposure 3D keeps a target SD for the chosen slice thickness, including AIDR 3D / AiCE. All four also modulate mA along z (curve on the AP scout).
  • Noise and contrast: σ ∝ kernel factor × (1 − IR noise reduction) / √(detected photons × slice thickness); detected photons scale with effective mAs, kV output and transmission exp(−μ(kV)·Dw). At equal CTDIvol, low kV is noisier in large patients (about +30% dose at 80 kV for the same noise at 25 cm, and about +95% at 35 cm). Iodine contrast is about 1.7× at 80 kV and 1.3× at 100 kV relative to 120 kV; bone contrast also rises at low kV.
  • Automatic kV: for each kV the mA that keeps the CNR of the standard protocol is found, with a limit on how much noise may rise (about ×2 for angiography, ×1.25 for contrast-enhanced organs, ×1.05–1.15 without contrast). The kV with the lowest CTDIvol is chosen; the protocol kV is left only for a saving of at least 5%, and a higher kV is used only when the tube cannot deliver the mA.
  • Scanner data (detector, rotation times, kV steps, mA/kW limits, pitch, IR options, nCTDIw, AEC behaviour) are approximate public values for teaching; some items are options or depend on software version. Reference levels are the 75th percentile of per-scanner medians from anonymised multi-centre data, for teaching comparison only.
  • Size-specific reference levels: U.S. data from 2025 by water-equivalent diameter (Kanal KM et al., Radiology 2026;320(1):e260322). They are shown for the protocols that match one of the ten examination categories of that study; AD is the median and DRL the 75th percentile of facility medians, per acquisition.
  • Images and scouts are mathematical phantoms, not patient images. Not for clinical or regulatory use.
